Use an actual small Android phone and the office browser. Test 320px layout, enlarged text, numeric keyboard, labelled controls and visible focus. Time a normal entry after account selection; target under 15 seconds. Test directory search with at least 820 **synthetic** account rows; preserve IDs/numbers with leading zeros and same-name accounts.

Walk through agent, viewer and owner flows with separate accounts. Confirm no cross-agent access or owner financial access, correct independent check/undo behaviour, and that every saved/voided line has the expected cash effect. Verify stale data is visibly labelled and displayed totals state their scope.

Exercise 01:59:59 → 02:00 Kampala, month/year rollover, an open review sheet crossing the boundary, and reconnecting after midnight. Old sessions must reject writes; office checks remain available historically. Verify a second opening attempt resumes the same session.

Disconnect after caching, navigate every shell route, search saved records, select an uncached date, and confirm every mutation is disabled and never auto-sent on reconnect. Test browser refresh with a pending submission ID and check it before retrying. Test logout with multiple open tabs, blocked cache clearing, a shared-device memory-cache login and revoked accounts. Never claim remote erasure of already disconnected data.

Test CSV preview/apply with the three agreed columns, repeated names, leading zeros, conflicting numbers, invalid rows, same-file re-upload and a failed chunk. Confirm file omissions do not delete accounts and historical entry snapshots stay unchanged.

Before production, verify both projects' IDs/credentials independently, backup/export readiness, a documented late-correction process, the 100 million UGX cap, and username/password reset steps. Run a controlled notebook-parallel pilot; compare every office Ssentezo entry and resolve discrepancies before retiring paper.
